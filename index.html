Our Story

Pho House is family-founded. The kitchen follows traditional recipes, uses fresh ingredients, and takes care with time-honored techniques — so the food tastes the way it should.

The goal is simple: bring authentic Vietnamese flavors to Germantown, and be a community gathering place where friends and families can sit down together.
