Build your pho — a quick guide to the cuts

Every pho starts with slow-simmered beef broth and rice noodles, finished with herbs, bean sprouts and lime. Pick your cuts:

  • Steak (Eye Round): thin-sliced, lean, cooks in the hot broth.
  • Brisket: rich and tender, slow-cooked.
  • Flank: hearty and beefy.
  • Fat Skirt Flank: extra-rich, for a deeper bowl.
  • Tendon: soft and silky texture.
  • Tripe: classic chew, traditional pho texture.
  • Meatball: springy beef meatballs.

Not sure? Start with #1 Dac Biet (the house combination) or #15 Eye Round Steak for a clean, simple bowl. There is no #10 on the menu — it goes from #9 to #11.

Good to know

Do you offer dine-in?

Yes — dine-in every day 10:00 AM – 8:00 PM.

How late can I order pickup or delivery?

Pickup and delivery orders run 10:00 AM – 7:50 PM daily.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes — veggie pho, veggie garden rolls, crispy veggie rolls and tofu banh mi. Ask about fish sauce and broth if you eat strictly vegetarian.

How does the Banh Mi Buy 5 Get 1 Free deal work?

Buy five banh mi to-go and the sixth is free. Join Rewards to track stamps digitally — one stamp per banh mi ordered.

Do you cater?

Yes — pho bars, banh mi platters and spring-roll platters. See the Catering page for a quote.

What areas do you deliver to?

Germantown, Gaithersburg, Clarksburg, Montgomery Village, Boyds, Clopper, Middlebrook and nearby. Call if you are just outside the area.
